            Guardrails

            Before a document or skill is shared with the org it is scanned automatically for personal data and secrets: deterministic patterns, then a Gemini check that tells org-wide facts from personal values. If the Gemini check fails, the share is blocked (fail closed). The scan is a safety net, not a guarantee. Human review before publishing is planned (V3), not built. Findings are stored as categories only, never values.
